ABOUT WILD ROOTS

Small ideas can grow into something useful.

Wild Roots Collective Ltd is focused on building ideas with a practical purpose — projects that make everyday life a little more connected, useful and community-minded.

Based in New Zealand, we are interested in simple technology, local communities and projects that encourage people to share what they have and make better use of what is already around them.
