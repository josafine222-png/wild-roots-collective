SwapCrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

SwapCrop is a New Zealand app that helps people swap, give away and share homegrown produce, plants, seeds, eggs, herbs and homemade food with people nearby.

SwapCrop is operated by Wild Roots Collective Ltd ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SwapCrop, we may collect information you provide when you create and use your account, including:

  • Your name or display name
  • Email address
  • Profile information and profile photo, if you choose to provide one
  • Listings, photos and descriptions you create
  • Messages and offers you send or receive through SwapCrop
  • Reviews and ratings you provide
  • Reports or other information you submit about users, listings or activity

We may also collect general location information that you choose to provide or that is needed to show nearby listings. We do not need your precise home address to use SwapCrop.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your SwapCrop account
  • Display your profile and listings to other users
  • Help users discover nearby swaps and giveaways
  • Enable messaging, offers and completed swaps
  • Allow users to leave reviews and ratings
  • Respond to reports and help maintain a safe community
  • Provide customer support
  • Operate, maintain and improve SwapCrop
  • Manage subscriptions and access to subscription features
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

3. Information Shared With Other Users

Some information is visible to other SwapCrop users because it is necessary for the app to work. This may include your display name, profile photo, listings, listing photos, approximate location information, reviews and other information you choose to include in your public profile or listings.

Messages and offers are shared with the users involved in those conversations or swaps.

Please avoid including your home address, phone number, financial information or other sensitive personal information in public listings or messages.

4. Service Providers

We use trusted third-party service providers to help operate SwapCrop. These providers may process information on our behalf and are only authorised to use information as necessary to provide their services.

SwapCrop uses Supabase for services including account authentication, database storage and related app functionality.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

SwapCrop is intended to operate as a subscription service. Subscription payments may be processed through the relevant Apple App Store or Google Play payment systems.

We do not receive or store your full credit or debit card details when payments are processed through those platforms. Payment information is handled according to the privacy policies of Apple and Google.

6. Advertising and Tracking

SwapCrop does not currently display advertising and does not currently use third-party advertising trackers.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Safety and Reports

SwapCrop includes reporting and safety features. Information you submit through a report may be reviewed by us to investigate prohibited items, unsafe food, scams, harassment, illegal activity, unsafe situations or other issues.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SwapCrop, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce our rules and meet legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

10. Deleting Your Account

You can request deletion of your SwapCrop account through the account deletion feature in the app.

When your account is deleted, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify your personal information, subject to information we may be required or permitted to retain for legal, safety, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

11. Children

SwapCrop is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Your Privacy Rights

New Zealand privacy law gives individuals rights regarding their personal information. Depending on the circumstances, you may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct inaccurate information.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
